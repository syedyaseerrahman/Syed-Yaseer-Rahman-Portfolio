The Story

How I work, in practice

I joined an EdTech company in Bengaluru at a point where growth had outpaced process. Reporting lived in scattered spreadsheets, exceptions piled up faster than anyone could track them, and nobody had time to fix the machine while running it. I built a KPI governance framework to change that. Starting small, on four projects, then scaling it across more than twenty. It cut delivery exceptions from over 20 per cycle to under 5, and it gave the organisation back more than 600 hours a year that used to disappear into manual reporting.

The work went beyond the system itself. I coordinated with team leads and developers to keep it running day to day, led and mentored a small team, and worked directly with client accounts, from pitch materials through to ongoing relationship management. I also tracked variance and root-cause analysis that fed into real capital and resourcing decisions. In a UK contract role since, I've applied the same attention to reconciliation and discrepancy investigation, keeping operational records accurate to a strict standard.

The same instinct shows up outside of work too. I build small tools for myself when something repetitive is wasting time, including a daily Gmail digest that summarises what actually needs my attention each morning, because sorting an inbox by hand felt like exactly the kind of problem worth solving properly.

I'm currently looking for roles in operations, program coordination, delivery, and analysis, wherever the work is real and hands-on.
